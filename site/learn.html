Learning path · XiangShan alignment

Treat the 6502 as atrainer aircraft. A 12-week practice plan

The goal is not a more complete NES. It is to take a chip small enough to finish, end to end, and turn XiangShan's verification inner loop, CI gates and backend flow into muscle memory.

01Why 6502

One story, three loops.

XiangShan states its goal as "an agile development process for high-performance processors": a reference model, differential verification, generated descriptions, continuous regression, plus a roughly six-month iteration and tapeout cycle. The 6502 is small enough that one person can walk the whole chain.

Inner loop · minutes Lockstep DiffTest: change one line of RTL, run one command, see PASS or the first divergence. The most valuable — and hardest to self-teach — part of the method. Start with DiffTest → Middle loop · every commit CI regression, golden hashes, deterministic double runs, trace upload on failure. Without the middle loop the inner loop is just a script, not a gate. See verification → Outer loop · every iteration Synthesis, FPGA and open-PDK PPA drills. Skip the 55nm chase; get from RTL to an area report first. See the outer loop →

0312 weeks

Twelve weeks, raise the score.

Ordered by learning return: close the inner loop first, then add gates, then touch generation and backend. The pace is a suggestion, not a promise.

Do not skip steps: Chisel before DiffTest gives you two implementations you cannot trust; backend before gates mistakes "synthesises" for "correct". Until the inner loop closes, no outer-loop point counts.
04What transfers

What you keep, what you don't.

The 6502 gives you process muscle, not microarchitecture taste. Knowing the difference keeps you from drifting into NES peripherals.

Transfers to XiangShanDoes not transfer
The full mental model of reference + differential verificationOoO, speculation, branch prediction
Generated / parameterised hardware constructionCache coherence, multi-core, NoC
CI, regression, golden hashes, deterministic replayReal PPA optimisation (the 6502 is too small)
Checkpoints, replay, first-divergence localisationDesign taste for high-performance microarchitecture
Checklist thinking for backend and tapeout gatesCommercial PDK / DFT / sign-off engineering

Next stage: switch the vehicle to a small RISC-V core (NutShell is XiangShan's predecessor) or read XiangShan's Chisel source directly. The 6502's job is to shorten the loop before you switch.
