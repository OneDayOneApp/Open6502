Arrows or WASD to move, Z or Space for A, X for B, Enter for Start, Shift for Select, P to pause, R to restart, M for sound, F for fullscreen, F2 to save a state, F3 to return to the last one, L to rotate. Fullscreen blocks browser shortcuts, menus and page gestures until you leave it with Esc or the fullscreen button. Gamepads work too. Picture and sound come from the GEM6502 WebAssembly core in real time; the ROM is loaded straight from the catalog's download URL.
